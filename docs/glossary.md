# OSP Glossary

## Outside Plant (OSP)
The physical network between the exchange and the customer: cables, ducts, manholes, cabinets and splitters.

## Manhole
An underground chamber that gives access to ducts and houses cable joints or splitters.

## Duct and sub-duct
A duct is an underground pipe that carries cables between manholes. Sub-ducts are smaller pipes inside a duct, each usually holding one cable.

## Splitter
A passive optical device that splits one fibre into several outputs (for example 1:32) to serve multiple premises.

## NRI, GIS and DFF
NRI (Network Resource Inventory) records network assets and their attributes. GIS stores where assets are on the map. DFF (Digital Field Force) manages field work orders.
